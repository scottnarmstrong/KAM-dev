The v2 form of the sharp isoenergetic a-posteriori theorem (SHARP-PLUS), as an instance of track~K's v2 part (\texttt{blueprint/sharp2.tex},

concluding the frozen body \texttt{IG1Body}. Parts~0--2 of the track-I blueprint and its sharp part are included for the references; the new
part is the last section.
