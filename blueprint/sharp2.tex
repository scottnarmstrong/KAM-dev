\title{The v2 a-posteriori KAM theorem at fixed frequency: blueprint}
\author{Scott Armstrong and Zohar Komargodski}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
The v2 form of the sharp a-posteriori theorem (SHARP-PLUS): sharper small-divisor data (full-strip Bessel, ordered divisors, exact Abel
summation, frequency-specific constants at the bites), refined certificate data, the modified Newton step of Figueras--Haro with a stage-0
anisotropic weight, a stage-dependent Newton engine, and the hybrid theorem with a measured first step. Parts~0--4 of the main blueprint and the
sharp part are included for the references; the new parts are the last two sections.
\end{abstract}
