\title{The v2 a-posteriori KAM theorem with a certified first step: blueprint}
\author{Scott Armstrong and Zohar Komargodski}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
The hybrid form of the v2 sharp a-posteriori theorem (package SHARP2-HYBRID): a proved v2 branch theorem is restarted at the
torus $K_1=K+\Delta K$ of a first step computed outside the checker, and its conclusion is transferred back to $K$. Every hypothesis
for $K_1$ follows from explicit bounds on the step, on the linearised new error and on certified data of $K$. Parts~0--4 of the main
blueprint, the sharp part and the v2 parts are included for the references; the new part is the last section.
\end{abstract}
