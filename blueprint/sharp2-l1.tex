\title{The v2 a-posteriori KAM theorem with the weighted-$\ell^1$ entry step: blueprint}
\author{Scott Armstrong and Zohar Komargodski}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
The v2 form of the sharp a-posteriori theorem (SHARP-PLUS) and its weighted-$\ell^1$ hybrid (package B10): the first Newton step solves its
cohomological equations from weighted-$\ell^1$ data of the initial torus with the sup-over-modes small-divisor constant $\Gamma_\infty$, every other
estimate stays in sup norms. Parts~0--4 of the main blueprint, the sharp part and the v2 parts are included for the references; the new part is the
last section.
\end{abstract}
