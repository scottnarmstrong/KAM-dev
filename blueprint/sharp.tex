\author{Scott Armstrong and Zohar Komargodski}

\begin{abstract}
A sharper explicit form of the a-posteriori theorem G1 (Part~2 of the main blueprint): general small-divisor data
(R\"ussmann's exponent $\tau$ as the headline instance), the torsion route of Haro--Luque without a factor $1/\delta$, a
loss of $3\delta$ per step, constants in the separate sizes $M,\sigma_K,\sigma_B,\sigma_T$, and one abstract Newton engine
shared with the isoenergetic theorem. Parts~0--4 of the main blueprint are included for the references; the new part is
the last section.
\end{abstract}
