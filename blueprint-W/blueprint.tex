\begin{abstract}
A proof plan for track W: two-frequency quasi-periodic solutions of the cubic Dirichlet wave equation
$u_{tt}=u_{xx}-mu-u^3$ on $[0,\pi]$ for every fixed $m>0$, excited in the modes $J=\{1,2\}$ (owner decision D30).
Part~0 fixes every normalization once and proves milestone W0: the cubic attachment, the quartic coefficients, the
partial Birkhoff normal-form action coefficients, nondegeneracy for every $m>0$, and the Duffing consistency check.
The proposed headline W-NLW2 closes the document. Nothing here is frozen or approved. The milestone plan is in
\texttt{PLAN.md}.
\end{abstract}
