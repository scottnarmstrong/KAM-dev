# SO-DATA3 handoff and audit request: the N = 65 lunar theorems through the v2 certifier

Branch `inp-sodata3`. Nothing is frozen, and no landed declaration changed.

The two final theorems are ordinary theorems with the standard three axioms:
- `lunar_barriers_65_v2`;
- `lunar_spin_confinement_65_v2`.

Every heavy theorem was kernel-checked alone under a capped probe lock: 10 GB on lane 1, 8 GB on lane 2 (10 GB, serialized with lane 1, for the modules measured above 8 GB).

## 1. The final theorems (`KAM/SpinOrbit/LunarFinalR2V2.lean`)

```lean
theorem lunar_barriers_65_v2 :
    ∃ (Bm : SoBarrier lunarR2BandLo lunarR2BandHi) (Bp : SoBarrier lunarR2BandLo lunarR2BandHi),
      (∀ q φ, Bm.P q φ < Bp.P q φ) ∧
      (∀ q φ, Bm.P q φ < lunarR2PLo) ∧ (∀ q φ, (lunarR2PHi : ℝ) < Bp.P q φ)

theorem lunar_spin_confinement_65_v2 (q₀ p₀ φ₀ : ℝ)
    (hp : (lunarR2PLo : ℝ) < p₀ ∧ p₀ < lunarR2PHi) :
    ∃ z : ℝ → ℝ × ℝ × ℝ, (z 0 = (q₀, p₀, φ₀) ∧ ∀ t, HasDerivAt z (soField (z t)) t) ∧
      (∀ t, |(z t).2.1 - 1| ≤ (lunarR2Delta : ℝ)) ∧
      ∀ y : ℝ → ℝ × ℝ × ℝ, (y 0 = (q₀, p₀, φ₀) ∧ ∀ t, HasDerivAt y (soField (y t)) t) → y = z
```

**How they are proved:**
- `lunar_barriers_65_v2` is one application of `lunar_barriers_of_R2V2`, which is `routeAV2` at the refined-route
  witnesses through `checkTorusCertificateV2_sound`.
- Its band inequalities are closed by `decide +kernel` on the graph literals.
- `lunar_spin_confinement_65_v2` comes from `lunar_spin_confinement_of_band`.

**`#print axioms`** gives `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` for each of:
- both final theorems;
- `lower_passes`, `upper_passes`, `lowerGraph_passes`;
- `lunar_barriers_of_R2V2`.

## 3. The data and the proof

**v2 sizing (SO-V2PKG d01b):**
- L = 20, CertFixed 96, cut-offs 100000 / 160, budgets 1e-20 / 1e-8.
- kp 3, ϑ 1/8, J 20.
- Certified bites at δ₀ (l 11), δ₁ (12), b₀, b₁, b₂.

**Witness.** The witness numbers come from exact mirrors:
- `checkHamClaim`, `checkFrameMajorants` (`rowCornerLeq` with its foldr order);

**Heavy sub-checks: the segmented proof (SO-CSPLIT route E, SO-CSEG-GEN):**
- Generated by `scripts/spin_orbit/csplit_{plan,ledger,emit}.py`.
- B = 200 k census calls per theorem.
- **Composition chains:** 18 cfgFull segments (forced one-op cuts at slots 10 and 28) and 26 cfgLow segments
  (cut at 28) per torus.
- **Ledgers:** 67 cfgFull and 214 cfgLow models, each with its cached norm. Every segment was self-checked bit
  for bit by a shadow re-run in the mirror.
- **Packed products:** 128 evaluated per torus. Each has a shard theorem, a decode theorem and an assembly
  theorem; every side check is its own theorem.
- **Gram:** 3 entries, 4 BG entries and the rows.
- **Torsion:** 54 matrix-product entries, the stages, then `torsionModels_eq_of_stages` and
  `frameNModels_eq_of_stage`, as explicit instantiations (design R5).
- **Tails:** the energy tail, 4 invariance rows, and the frames N and T.

**Graph check.**
- Whole `checkSoGraph` exceeds 10 GB, so it is split into its four conjuncts.
- The pLo and pHi sides square a 271–325-term SOS witness by `directItems`. They are split by 32-row chunks.
  The new module `KAM/SpinOrbit/Data/CsegGraph.lean` proves that the landed balanced merge tree, over chunks of
  2^5 rows, is the chunk-wise tree (`levelNCseg_flatten`, by induction).
- The chunk literals equal the mirror's whole `directItems` (asserted before emission).

**New Lean, all computation-only lemmas under new names, nothing assumed about data:**

## 6. Design signals (no limit raised)

1. **`decide +kernel` on a large inline term.**
   - It fails in the kernel, and the fallback diagnostic then exhausts the heartbeats.
   - It works when the statement is made of named constants.
   - Remedy: entry theorems decide `segBeq` on a named right side (`…Rhs`).
2. **Nested `decide`s inside one declaration share one heartbeat budget.**
   - Remedy: every side check is its own theorem.
3. **Whole `checkSoGraph` and the SOS square are over 10 GB.**
   - Remedy: the conjunct split, then the row-chunk split (`CsegGraph`).
4. **Elaborator `isDefEq` against implicit arguments of `mulPacked_eq_of_parts` timed out.**
   - Remedy: every implicit argument is given (R5).
