# A kernel-checked KAM torus of the secular Sun–Jupiter–Saturn model (side project LG00)

**Status:** in progress, 97 % (status file reviewed 2026-11-02 03:25 UTC): the two tori and the confinement theorem
are proved in Lean on side-campaign branches; the independent audit is partly done and the separate repository is
pending · no frozen ids (side project; nothing in the main library's manifest) · axioms of the confinement theorem,

## In brief

Over millions of years the orbits of Jupiter and Saturn slowly change shape and orientation. Locatelli and Giorgilli
(2000) studied a "secular" model of this slow motion, obtained by averaging over the fast orbital revolutions: a
Hamiltonian system with two degrees of freedom, a polynomial in the eccentricity–perihelion variables of the two
planets. With computer-assisted estimates they proved that the motion starting from the actual Jupiter–Saturn data
is trapped forever between two invariant tori. LG00 reproduces the KAM part of that result in Lean: both tori are
verified by Lean's kernel through the certificate checker of [track SO, part 1](SO-1.md), and a Lean theorem proves the
confinement. To our knowledge it is the first formally verified KAM torus of a planetary model. It is a formal
reproduction of a published result, not new mathematics.

## Exactly what is proved

The Lean code is **not in this repository**. It lives on side-campaign branches (`lg00-prep`, `lg00-cert`, and an
application package `applications/lg00`), outside the library's default build (D51). The description below follows
the campaign's records ([`lg00-status.json`](../campaign/side/lg00-status.json) and the reports cited there); the exact
Lean statements are not reproduced here.

### The model

LG00's eq. (11): a polynomial H₁₁(ξ₁, ξ₂, η₁, η₂) with 69 nonzero monomials of degrees 2, 4 and 6, all even in η,
with exact decimal coefficients from the paper (its additive constant dropped). The variables are Poincaré-type
eccentricity–perihelion variables of Jupiter and Saturn, ξ = √(2P) cos p, η = √(2P) sin p with p = −ϖ and
P = Λ(1 − √(1 − e²)); η is the coordinate and ξ the momentum. Units: AU, years, G = 1. In Lean the model is
`lg00H11` with its vector field `lg00H11Field`; the certificate uses action–angle variables through a square-root
chart (`lg00Psi`, identity `lg00_ham_identity`), so the certified Hamiltonian is exactly (11), not a polynomial
approximation of it.

### The results (as recorded)

1. **First torus** (`lg00_kamTorus`, `lg00_physical_torus`, branch `lg00-cert`): a passing v2 certificate gives an
   exact invariant torus of the model with a frequency vector on LG00's noble direction Γ′ (eq. (51)), Diophantine
   with τ = 1 (Lean lemmas `isDiophantine_rP`, `isDiophantine_rPP` for the two directions), on an energy level E₀
   about 10⁻¹² (relative) from the energy of LG00's initial point. 5,183 kernel modules, 31.4 CPU-hours. The statement
   is byte-identical to an independently audited draft (AUDIT-LG00-STMT-1).
2. **T′ at the exact energy E\*** of LG00's initial point (Table 3): proved twice, by the original route (5,241
   modules, 29.9 CPU-h) and by the packed route (881 modules, 2.84 CPU-h).
3. **T″**, a second torus on the same energy level E\*, on the direction Γ″: proved by the packed route (592 modules,
   1.75 CPU-h).
4. **Confinement** (`LG00.Confinement.lg00_confinement`, built 2026-11-01 22:26 UTC): a theorem with no hypotheses
   stating that the solution of model (11) from LG00's Table 3 initial data exists for all time, is unique, stays on
   its energy level, has its normal-mode actions in a certified box, and stays strictly between the certified tori
   T′ and T″ forever. An independent walk over 96,288 constants found only the three standard axioms.

On an energy level of a two-degree-of-freedom system (a 3-dimensional set), invariant 2-tori separate the level, which
is why two tori on the level of the initial point trap that orbit.

## What is assumed and what is not claimed

- **Secular model, not the Solar System.** The theorem concerns LG00's truncated, averaged polynomial model (11),
  with its coefficients taken as exact. It says nothing directly about the full Newtonian three-body problem, about
  the fast (orbital-period) variables, or about the influence of the other planets. Moving closer to the physical
  system is the subject of other tracks (for example SJSU).
- **A reproduction.** The KAM part of LG00's Theorem 1 is reproduced; LG00's own proof also used rigorous numerics.
  Tori at two to three times LG00's eccentricities were found numerically to converge, but are not certified.
- **Not yet final.** The audit of the confinement theorem (AUDIT-LG00-CONF-PACKED-1) passed *with changes*: it
  proposed additions to the statement for the owner, and packaging changes. The separate public repository and its
  self-contained build are pending. Until then the statement wording may still change.
- **Not in the main library.** The certificate never enters this repository's default build, and it does not count
  toward the main project's line or theorem counts.

route A; later the packed jet route, which cut the kernel cost about tenfold), with the frequency's Diophantine
property proved in Lean from the continued-fraction structure of the noble ratios; confinement between T′ and T″ uses
graph adapters of the certificate (angle-graph and action-range checks) and the uniqueness of solutions. Records: the
