# HD 11964: a certified no-orbit-crossing time for an exoplanet system (side project)

**Status:** in progress (about 51 % by the side orchestrator's stage weights, last reviewed 2026-11-02; this page
reflects `main` on 2026-11-07) · **not proved** · no frozen statement: the headline theorem is a design proposal,
not an owner-approved frozen anchor · the Lean pieces listed as proved below use the standard axioms only
(`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`), according to the stage records

## In brief

HD 11964 is a star with two known planets on wide orbits: c, close in (period 37.9 days), and b, far out
(period 1935 days, about 51 times longer). The goal is a machine-checked theorem saying that, in the Newtonian
point-mass model of this system built from catalogue data, the two orbits cannot cross and neither planet can approach
the star for a fixed time T (first target T = 10³ years), for every initial position of the planets along their
orbits and every orientation of the ellipses. The method is the one behind track LP ([LP.md](LP.md)), made
quantitative: certify by computer that the semi-major-axis actions drift by less than a fixed amount over time T;
exact conservation of angular momentum then keeps the angular momentum deficit below the strong threshold, and the
sealed barrier theorem LP0 turns this into separated orbits. The general bridge and many components are proved in
Lean; the computer-checked certificate that supplies the drift bounds has not been produced, so the headline theorem
does not exist yet.

## Exactly what is proved, and what is the target

### The model (fixed data)

- **Model:** the planar moving-star Hamiltonian of tracks P and LP, `movingHamiltonian 1 1 ν μ`
  (heliocentric positions, rescaled barycentric momenta, G = 1, star mass 1; see [LP.md](LP.md#setting-and-definitions)),
  with planet 0 = c (inner) and planet 1 = b (outer).
- **Masses** (exact rationals from the catalogue decimals, `HD11964.Threshold`):
  μ = m_b/M⋆ = 2005497248200/3833351025484041 ≈ 5.2317·10⁻⁴ and ν = (1217282799/10027486241, 1) ≈ (0.121395, 1).
  The masses are minimum masses m sin i (Rosenthal et al. 2021, ApJS 255, 8, via the NASA Exoplanet Archive,
  1 November 2026), with coplanar orbits seen edge-on.
- **Units:** lengths in au; time unit 1 yr/(2π√1.151343) ≈ 0.148326 yr.
- **Initial set:** canonical states (q_i ≠ 0, E_i < 0) with positive total angular momentum L > 0, Kepler actions
  Λ ∈ [Λlo, Λhi] with Λlo = (116621/2000000, 4454241/2500000), Λhi = (292553/5000000, 3575393/2000000)
  (around the nominal Λ̂ ≈ (0.05841, 1.78470)), and strong AMD C = ΣΛ_i − |L| ≤ 1/100 (nominal C ≈ 0.009455). All
  mean longitudes and perihelion directions are allowed; the eccentricities are limited only through the AMD bound.

### Target theorem (designed, not proved)

The proposed headline (`HD11964.no_crossing`, design §2) reads: there are d*, d_pair > 0 such that every initial
state z₀ in the initial set has a moving solution on [−T, T], unique there, along which for every t ∈ [−T, T]

```
a_i(1 − e_i) ≥ d*,   a_b(1 − e_b) − a_c(1 + e_c) ≥ d_pair,   |q_i| ≥ d*,   |q_c − q_b| ≥ d_pair,
```

with T an explicit rational, first T = 6742 time units (at least 1000 years), then possibly 10⁶ and 10⁹ years. The
constants d*, d_pair are LP0's, existential and not explicit. Only frozen definitions occur in the statement; it has
not been submitted for owner approval or frozen.

### Proved in the core library on `main` ([`KAM/Application/LambdaDrift/`](../../KAM/Application/LambdaDrift))

- **No crossing from a certified action drift, for any planar system**
  ([`noCrossing_of_approxIntegrals`](../../KAM/Application/LambdaDrift/NoCrossing.lean)). Fix n ≥ 2, ν_i > 0, a
  compact set U of actions in the chamber and δ > 0. There are d*, d_pair > 0 (LP0's constants for U and margin δ/2)
  such that for all rational drift data (η_i, lo_i, hi_i, T, d, r) with T > 0, d > 0, 2d ≤ r and
  η_i T + (hi_i − lo_i) < d (decided by computation), every μ, every initial state z₀ satisfying the tube condition
  `LiftData ν U z₀ δ r` (z₀ canonical with L(z₀) > 0, the closed max-norm ball of radius r around Λ(z₀) inside U,
  C(z₀) ≤ B(Λ(z₀)) − δ, and the budget n r + Ω_B(r) ≤ δ/4, where Ω_B is the modulus of continuity of the strong
  threshold on U), and every family of
  functions F_i on the Poincaré chart, differentiable on an open set O ⊃ R, where R contains every chart state whose
  actions lie within d of Λ(z₀) and whose AMD is at most C(z₀) + nd, with

  ```
  |dF_i(X_H)| ≤ η_i  and  lo_i ≤ F_i − Λ_i ≤ hi_i   on R      (X_H: the chart vector field of the exact Hamiltonian),
  ```

  the moving solution from z₀ exists and is unique on [−T, T] and keeps the four separations above. This is a
  conditional theorem: the bracket and range bounds on R are hypotheses, to be supplied by the certificate.
  Its parts: [`stoppedDrift_of_approxIntegrals`](../../KAM/Application/LambdaDrift/Bridge.lean) (the drift
  ‖Λ(t) − Λ(0)‖ ≤ d ≤ r/2 by a first-exit argument along the chart lift) and `noCrossing_of_stoppedDrift`, which
  composes LP's first-exit theorem with the sealed barrier LP0.
- **The order-2 Lie remainder identity** ([`LieRemainder.lean`](../../KAM/Application/LambdaDrift/LieRemainder.lean),
  [`LieRemainderChart.lean`](../../KAM/Application/LambdaDrift/LieRemainderChart.lean)): for
  F_i = Λ_i + ∂_{λ_i}χ₁ + ∂_{λ_i}χ₂ − ½{∂_{λ_i}χ₁, χ₁}, the rate dF_i(X_H) equals an explicit sum of five terms
  (two Fourier tails and three brackets), each to be bounded separately, so the certificate never has to cancel
  large terms. With the Poisson calculus ([`Poisson.lean`](../../KAM/Application/LambdaDrift/Poisson.lean),
  [`LieCalculus.lean`](../../KAM/Application/LambdaDrift/LieCalculus.lean)).
- **Rotation reduction** ([`Rotation.lean`](../../KAM/Application/LambdaDrift/Rotation.lean)): bounds on
  rotation representatives give bounds on the whole region, for rotation-invariant F.
- **Threshold tools** ([`Threshold.lean`](../../KAM/Application/LambdaDrift/Threshold.lean)): a monotone lower
  bound for B_ij, and `liftData_of_star` when the strong threshold is a star threshold Λ_k on U.
- **Reduced-mass observables** ([`ObservableNoCrossing.lean`](../../KAM/Application/LambdaDrift/ObservableNoCrossing.lean)
  and companions): the same conclusion with the first exit driven by reduced Delaunay actions, which removes the
  O(μ) oscillation of Λ_b from the certified range.

### Proved in the opt-in package [`applications/hd11964`](../../applications/hd11964) (on `main`, outside the core build)

- `HD11964.Threshold`: the action box U = [Λlo − r, Λhi + r] (r = 1/250) lies in the chamber, and on U the strong
  threshold is the star threshold of planet c, B(Λ) = Λ_c (the pair threshold is at least 0.785, against
  Λ_c ≤ 0.0626); every state of the initial set gives `LiftData` with δ = Λlo_c − 1/100 ≈ 0.0483.
- `HD11964.NoCrossing.noCrossing_of_regionBounds`: the target conclusion **given** the bracket and range bounds on
  the fixed region R(d) = {chart states with Λ ∈ [Λlo − d, Λhi + d], Γ_c + Γ_b ≤ 1/100 + 2d} and decided drift data;
  R(d) is rotation invariant and contains every reachable state. Also a version with reduced-mass observables
  (`NoCrossingObs`).
- `HD11964.Cover`, `HD11964.CoverPlan`: a 50-cell cover of the rotation representatives of R(1/500), checked by
  kernel computation.
- Pieces of the cell check: Cauchy bounds for slow derivatives (`CellCauchy`); the exact generator
  χ* = Π_S P/(i k·ω(Λ)) with a certified remainder (`Generator`); the order-2 homological equation and pure Fourier
  tails (`Homological`, `ChiSmooth`, `ChiHD`); rotation invariance of F (`ChiRot`); tail bounds from Fourier decay on
  strips (`TailBound`, `TailAniso`, `TailSum`); complex extensions of the perturbation (`Holo*`); and the glue from
  per-cell bounds to the region bounds (`Glue`, `ChiRot.noCrossing_HD_of_cellBounds`).

## What is assumed and what is not claimed

- When finished, the theorem will concern the stated planar Newtonian point-mass model with these exact rational
  parameters. It will say nothing about the true masses (sin i unknown), mutual inclination, tides, relativity, the
  stellar radius or other bodies, nor about observational uncertainty beyond the explicit initial set (the
  catalogue's eccentricity error bars are wider than that set; the action box covers about the 1σ period
  uncertainty of b).
- No orbit crossing means LP0's four separations for the canonical elements (velocity p_i/ν_i), not the osculating
  heliocentric ellipses; d* and d_pair are not explicit.
- Nothing is claimed after time T. The 10⁶- and 10⁹-year rungs are planned; the 10⁹-year rung is provisional (the
  order-4 normal form it needs is not resolved in double precision).
- The general Lean theorems above are conditional on certificate bounds; none of them is a statement about
  HD 11964 without those bounds.

Route. The approximate integrals F_i ≈ Λ_i come from an order-2 Lie normal form in the fast angles, with exact Fourier
projections onto a finite non-resonant mode set (all modes satisfy |k·ω| ≥ 0.176; the 51:1 near-resonance lies
outside the set). The certificate bounds |{F_i, H}| against the original Hamiltonian on each cell of the cover
through the five-term identity, and the range of F_i − Λ_i. If η_i T + (hi_i − lo_i) < d, the actions drift by less
than d up to time T; then C(t) − C(0) = Σ ΔΛ_i keeps C below B = Λ_c with margin, LP's first-exit theorem gives
existence and uniqueness on [−T, T], and LP0 gives the separations. The sampled numerical gate found margins of about
4.7 for the 10³- and 10⁶-year rungs, set by the oscillation of Λ_b rather than by the drift rate.
