## Approach (blueprint `bpI:p2s:scheme`, `bpI:p2:energy-limit`, `bpI:p2s:g1`, `g1-instances`)

A5's fixed-frequency instance (`KAM/Sharp/{Scheme,G1}.lean`) is the model throughout.

- **`isoScheme_isNewton`.**
  - **(N1)** comes from `IsoAdmissible`.
  - **(N2)**, steps `sc-s` and `sc-n2`:
    - The caps give `m_i ≤ σ_i`, since `μ ≥ 0` and the rates are `≥ 0`. So `iso_step_spec` applies to
      `(K, s)` admissible for `(σ_K, σ_B, σ_T)`.
    - **Ray.** `|s̄ − s||ω*| ≤ C_Δω U₄` and the fourth cap `m₄ + C_Δω U₄ ≤ ν/2` give `C_Δω U₄ < ν − m₄`.
      Then `KAM.Iso.Apost.ray` (b) gives `s̄ > 1` and `ν(s̄) ≥ ν − (m₄ + C_Δω U₄)`.
    - **New bounds.** They follow from the triangle inequality on the `ϱ_D`, `ϱ_B`, `ϱ_𝖳` increments
      (`norm_le_add_of_sub_le`).
    - **Parameter increment.** `|s̄|ω*| − s|ω*|| = |s̄ − s||ω*|`.
- **`iso_engine_hyps`** (step `g1-eng`). Every branch goes through one budget lemma, `iso_budget`: from `c X ≤ 1`
  and `4γ̄²q ≤ cB`, it gives `q y ≤ B/4` for `y ≤ γ̄²X`.
  - (I1), (I2) come from `2^{2a}C_E, ĉ_step ≤ ĉ_*`.
  - (I3) and the first three caps come from `ĉ_sum ≤ ĉ_* μ` and `absorb` (c).
  - The fourth cap comes from the branch `4γ̄²C_Δω/ν ≤ ĉ_*`.
- **`iso_energy_limit`.**
  - The engine (`iterate`, `limit`) runs from `x₀`. The iterates stay within `D_s ≤ μ/4` of `x₀.1`
    (`iso_iter_close`, by telescoping), and so does the limit.
  - So `tendsto_energyAverage_of_uniform` gives `Ē(K_s) → Ē(K_∞)`, while `‖Ē(K_s) − E₀‖ ≤ ε_s → 0`.
  - The two limits agree by uniqueness of limits.
- **`g1_gen`.**
  - **Standing data and initial state.** `IsoStanding` is built from the binders, as in `g1_explicit`.
    - `g1_error_small_aux` gives `e < 1` and `ĉ_* e/(γ⁴δ₀^{2a}) < 1`.
    - The initial state `(K, s)` with bounds `m⁽⁰⁾ = (σ_K − μ, σ_B − μ, σ_T − μ, 0)` is admissible:
      `norm_le_of_rowSum_le_of_nonempty` turns the row sums into norms, and `margin_of_le` gives `ν ≤ ν(s)`.
  - **`iso_g1_core`** runs the engine and reads off the limit:
    - **`g1-lim`.**
      - `s_∞ = p_∞/|ω*|`, and `s_j → s_∞` because `p_j → p_∞`.
      - `ν(s_j) ≥ ν − m_{j,4} ≥ ν/2`, and `ν(·)` is continuous (`continuous_rayMargin`), so `ν(s_∞) ≥ ν/2 > 0`;
        hence `1 < s_∞ < σ_ω`.
      - `|s_∞ω* − sω*| = |p_∞ − p₀| ≤ 2C_Δω e/(γ²δ₀^a)`.
    - **`g1-o`.**
      - Invariance at `s_∞ω*` is `lim_invariance_moving`.
      - The energy is `iso_energy_limit` followed by `energy_conservation` at `s_∞ω*`.
      - The margins come from `lim_margins`, and `|DK_∞| ≤ σ_K − μ/2` from the cap.
  - **Constants.** `g1_closeness_eq` gives `C₂♯`, `C₃♯`.
- **Instances.** `cohData_russmann`, `cohData_parseval` and `cohData_crude`, each with `τ ≥ n − 1 ≥ 1`.

## Uncertainties

- **Unused arguments.** `isoScheme_isNewton` calls `iso_consts_pos` with `μ := 1, ν := 1`. Those arguments are
  unused for the constants it needs, and its own signature has only `0 ≤ μ`. A5 did the same.
- **Implicit arguments.** The signature check compares explicit hypotheses in order. The order of the section
  implicits is that of the design's variable blocks, but it was not checked with `@`-equality; the three `def`s
  were.
- **Line wrapping.** The long signature line of `g1_gen` was wrapped to 100 characters. The statement is unchanged
  (the signature check was re-run afterwards).
