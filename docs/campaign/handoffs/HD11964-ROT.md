## Design

**(1) `hFrot`.** `F_i = approxIntegral₂ (castAdd i) χ₁ χ₂` with `χ₁ = χ*(P)`, `G₂ = {P − ½Π_S P, χ₁}`,
`χ₂ = χ*(G₂)`. The invariance goes in four steps.
- **`χ*` under the rotation (1a, 1b).** `withAngles (chartRot θ w) λ = chartRot θ (withAngles w (λ − θ))`.
  - So for a rotation-invariant `P` that is `2π`-periodic in the angles, the coefficient integrand at
    `chartRot θ w` is the one at `w`, shifted by `θ/2π` in both variables and multiplied by `e(−k·(θ/2π)𝟙)`.
  - The double integral of a `1`-periodic function is shift-invariant (`Function.Periodic.intervalIntegral_add_eq`,
    no integrability needed).
  - Hence `P̂_k(chartRot θ w) = e(−k·(θ/2π)𝟙) P̂_k(w)`, while `e(k·λ/2π)` gains `e(k·(θ/2π)𝟙)` and the divisor
    depends on `Λ` only. So `χ*` and `Π_S P` are invariant.
  - **No condition on `S` is needed for invariance.** Periodicity of `χ*` is automatic: the coefficients do not
    see the angles, and the characters are `1`-periodic.
- **Brackets (1c, 1d).** `chartRot θ` and `· + fastShift m` are affine with symplectic linear parts. The core lemma
  `fderiv_apply_hamiltonianVectorField_comp` (Rotation.lean) gives:
  - invariance of `pb a b` for invariant `a`, `b` on an invariant open set;
  - with `A (angleDir k) = angleDir k` and `(R y).2 k = y.2 k`, invariance of `D_k χ`, `{D_k χ₁, χ₁}`, hence of
    `approxIntegral₂`.
- **`P` (1e).** `chartPert = liftChartHam − hProd`:
  - `liftChartHam_chartRot` and `poincareProd_lattice` give rotation invariance and periodicity;
  - `hProd` depends on `Λ` only.
- **HD (1f, 1g).**
  - `openHD` is rotation- and lattice-invariant (`Λ`, `chartAMD` fixed).
  - `P`, then `χ₁`, then `P − ½Π_S P` and `G₂`, then `χ₂`, then `F_i` are invariant on `openHD`.
  - (1g) is `noCrossing_of_cellBounds` with `O = openHD` and `F = FHD`. `hF` (`contDiffOn_chi_HD`), `hFrot` (1f),
    `O ⊆ chartSafe ν` and `R(D.d) ⊆ O` (from `D.d ≤ 1/500`) are discharged. **What stays as premises**: the cell
    bounds and the links of the layer's functions to `FHD`.

**(2) The tails.** On `U`, `r₁ = P − Π_S P` and `r₂ = G₂ − Π_S G₂` (CHI). So `T_j = D_i (Q − Π_S Q)` with `Q = P`
or `G₂`, and the bound is one statement about `Q`.
- **The slice at `w`** is `g(t) = Q(withAngles w (2πt))`, `C^∞` and `1`-periodic on `ℝ²`, with coefficients `Q̂_k(w)`
  (the iterated integral equals `KAM.torusCoeff` by Fubini).
- The coefficients of `∂_i g` are `2πi k_i Q̂_k(w)` (`KAM.torusCoeff_fderiv_coordinate_real`).
- **Decay (2a).** If `Qc` extends the slice holomorphically and periodically to the strip of width `ρ`, within `r`
  of a trigonometric polynomial `Σ_{k∈T} a_k e(k·θ)`, then `|Q̂_k − a_k| ≤ e^{−2πρ|k|₁} r` (`KAM.norm_torusCoeff_le`
  applied to the difference).
- **Inversion.** The derivative's coefficients are then summable, and `KAM.hasSum_torusCoeff_mul_character` writes
  `∂_i g(λ/2π)` as its Fourier series. Subtracting `∂_i` of the finite sum `Π_S` leaves the modes outside `S`.
- **Result (2c):** `|T| ≤ Σ_{k∈T∖S} |k_i| |a_k| + r Σ_{k∉S} |k_i| e^{−2πρ|k|₁}`. There is no `2π`:
  `D_i = ∂_{λ_i} = (2π)⁻¹ ∂_{θ_i}`.
- **Why the model form, and the isotropic strip.** The strip is `KAM.complexStrip` (the layer's `T_ρ`). With the
  explicit modes of the model outside `S` counted exactly and only the remainder `r` weighted by the decay, the
  bound does not lose the fast `k_b` decay (`0.116^{|k_b|}`, HD11964-PROD) to an isotropic `ρ` fitted to `k_c`
  (`e^{−0.30|k_c|}`).
- **Cell form (2d).** On a well-formed cell, with the same cell-model hypotheses as `abs_chiStar_sub_le` (holomorphy
  and periodicity in `θ`, the strip × polydisc model, the link at real points), for `T₁` (model of `P`) and `T₂`
  (model of `G₂`).
- **Closed form (2e).** For a box mode set, `tailWeight` is bounded by products of four one-dimensional sums,
  rational in a rational `q̄ ≥ e^{−2πρ}`.
- **The (2a) remainder with decay (2b, L6).** The same decay, applied to `Pc − p` inside `norm_genChi_sub_le`:
  - the weight of `r_P B_k` becomes `e^{2π(ρ'−ρ)|k|₁}` when the generator is evaluated on the strip of width `ρ'`;
  - the other two terms are unchanged.

**(3) The numbers.** A Python evaluation (8 GB cap) of the (2a) radius, with and without decay, on the worst cell,
from the producer's data. Then a verdict on the adapter of AUDIT-HD11964-LEMMAS-1 L2.

/-! ## (1) `hFrot`: the rotation invariance of `F` -/

/-- (1a) **Fourier coefficients under the chart rotation**: for `P` rotation-invariant and
`2π`-periodic in the angles on an angle-saturated `U`,
`P̂_k(chartRot θ w) = e(-k·(θ/2π, θ/2π)) P̂_k(w)`. -/
def ChartCoeffChartRot : Prop :=
  ∀ (U : Set (Phase (2 + 2))) (P : Phase (2 + 2) → ℝ) (k : Fin 2 → ℤ), IsAngleSaturated U →
    IsRotInvOn U P → IsAnglePeriodicOn U P → ∀ θ : ℝ, ∀ w ∈ U,
      chartCoeff P k (chartRot θ w) = charE (-k) (fun _ => ((θ / (2 * π) : ℝ) : ℂ)) * chartCoeff P k w

/-- (1b) **`χ*` and `Π_S P` are rotation-invariant** (no condition on `S`), and always `2π`-periodic
in the angles. -/

  ∀ (U : Set (Phase (2 + 2))) (S : Finset (Fin 2 → ℤ)) (frq : (Fin 2 → ℝ) → Fin 2 → ℝ)
    (P : Phase (2 + 2) → ℝ), IsAngleSaturated U → IsRotInvOn U P → IsAnglePeriodicOn U P →
    IsRotInvOn U (chiStar S frq P) ∧ IsRotInvOn U (projS S P) ∧
      IsAnglePeriodicOn univ (chiStar S frq P) ∧ IsAnglePeriodicOn univ (projS S P)

/-- (1c) **Brackets of invariant functions are invariant** under a map with symplectic surjective
derivative `A` that preserves an open set (`chartRot θ`, `· + fastShift m`). -/
