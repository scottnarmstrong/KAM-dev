## Approach (blueprint `bpI:p2s:correction`, `newton-error`, `twist`, `step`)

- **`iso_correction_bounds`.**
  - **Data** (`co-data`):
    - `H_L`, `H_N` come from `frame_bounds`;
    - `H_Y = Mσ_Kσ_B` comes from `extended_basic` at `(K, sω*)`, where `K` is admissible and `s > 1`, so the
      `Setting` holds;
    - `H_T = C_T0 + 1` holds on `T_{r-δ}` by `torsion_bound` together with `hTS`;
    - `|ω| ≤ 2nσ_Kσ_B(M + ε) ≤ Θ` comes from `frequency`.
  - **Bordered system.** `border_bounds` (A6A) gives the sizes. `border_solve` is used only for its qualitative
    clauses (a), (b).
  - **Arithmetic** (`co-s`, `co-xi`, `co-om`) is done in `u = ε/(γδ^κ)` and `E = ε/(γ²δ^{2κ})`, with `absorb`.
  - **`co-dk`.**
    - `∂_ω L = AL − DE` (`fderiv_tangentMat_eq`).
    - `∂_ω N = Ω₀(AL−DE)B − N((AL−DE)ᵀL + Lᵀ(AL−DE))B` (`torsion_identity` (b)).
    - With `|DE| ≤ nε/δ ≤ n` and `|(DE)ᵀ| ≤ 2n` (`geo_de`, `ε ≤ δ`), `|∂_ω P| ≤ C_P` holds exactly, with no `1/δ`.
  - **Core lemma.** The proof is `correction_core`, which also exports the `ξᴸ` bound that `C_E2` needs. The
    target is its first conjunct.
- **`iso_newton_error_bounds`.**
  - **Identities.** They are those of `KAM.Iso.Apost.newton_error`, rebuilt from its helpers, which carry no
    constants: `sumVec_invarianceError_isoNewtonMap`, `isoResidual_eq_frame`, `reduced`, `energy_defect`, and
    the qualitative conjuncts of `border_facts` and `KAM.Iso.Apost.correction`.
    - The old `sMat_bounds`, `sumVec_error_eq` and `energyAverage_sub_eq` take hypotheses in the old constants.
    - The copies `sumVec_error_eq_of` and `energyAverage_sub_eq_of` take `det S ≠ 0` and `|ΔK| < β` pointwise
      instead.
    - `|S⁻¹| ≤ 2` is `NewtonError.sMat_spec` applied to `geometry_sep`'s `E_sym` bound and `hsym`.
  - **Invariance error.** `E_lin` is bounded through `iso_lin_eq`; then Taylor with `M/2`, and `iso_total_le`
    (`absorb` (d)) gives `C_E1`.
  - **Energy error.** `|D(H∘K)| ≤ n(|ηᴺ| + |ω||Ω_K|)` at real points (`energy_defect`), times the `ξᴸ` bound,
    plus the Taylor remainder with `M/2`. `energy_total_le` (`absorb` (c) applied to `ε²`, and (d), which is
    where `κ ≥ 1` enters) gives `C_E2`.
- **`iso_twist`.** Audit finding ISO-1 is followed. At the new pair `(K̃, s̃ω*)` the proof uses only:
  - `perturbation_poly` at `ω' = s̃ω*`;
  - `LastRowAux.energyRow_eq_sub_etaL` (whose only premise is Gram invertibility, given by `perturbation_poly`
    (b) on `T_{r-3δ}`);
  - `extended_regular` (continuity of the new bordered torsion at the arbitrary frequency `s̃ω*`).

  It uses neither `last_row` nor `extended_basic`, and nothing about `s̃ > 1`. The steps:
  - **`tw-t`:** `|T̃ − T| ≤ twistD ≤ C_ΔT U` at every real point (`twistD_le_iCDT`).
  - **`tw-y`:** `∑|Ỹ − Y| ≤ n|ω̃ − ω| + n|η̃ᴸ| + n|ηᴸ|` (`sum_norm_energyRow_sub_le_eta`), with
    `|ηᴸ| ≤ 2nσ_Kσ_Bε` and `|η̃ᴸ| ≤ 2nϑ̂²σ_Kσ_B ε̃` (`norm_etaL_le_of_le`).
  - **`tw-avg`:** `TorsionDiff.norm_extendedTorsion_sub_le` at each real point gives
    `max{C_ΔT U + C_Δω E, nC_Δω E + 2n²(1+ϑ̂²)σ_Kσ_Bε} ≤ C_Δ𝖳 U`, and then
    `TorsionDiff.norm_extendedTorsionAverage_sub_le` passes to the averages.
  - **`tw-inv`:** `neumann_ratio` with `q = ϑ`, so `ϑ̂σ_T²C_Δ𝖳 U = ϱ_𝖳 U`.
- **`iso_step_spec`.**
  - `iso_step_small` gives the smallness consequences.
  - `iso_correction_bounds` and `iso_newton_error_bounds` give (b) and (c).
  - (a) uses the qualitative clauses of `KAM.Iso.Apost.correction` and the `TorsionAux.isoNewtonMap_*` helpers.
  - The ball and the frame (d) come from `perturbation_poly` at `s̄ω*`, with `n d/δ = ϱ_D U` and `C_B d/δ = ϱ_B U`
    (`StepSharp.div_delta_eq`).
  - (e) is `iso_twist` with `K̃ = K̄`, `s̃ = s̄`, and `|ω̄ − ω| ≤ C_Δω E` from the correction.

## Uncertainties

- **Implicit arguments.** The implicit-argument order follows the signatures file's variable blocks. The check
  applies only the explicit hypotheses; it was not checked with `@`-equality.
- **Twist average.** `iso_twist` bounds the bordered difference pointwise and then averages it, instead of
  averaging the three blocks separately as step `tw-avg` describes. The bound and the constant are the same.
  `ExtendedAux.extendedTorsionAverage_eq_bordMat`, which design §7 names, is therefore not needed. Its
  continuity input, `extended_regular`, is used.
- **Generic copies.** The design's §2.2 copies are public helpers under distinct names, not `private`. Each
  states its pointwise hypothesis explicitly, and none stands in for a source result. The third copy
  (`sMat_bounds`) is not a separate lemma: one application of `NewtonError.sMat_spec` replaces it.
- **Old lemmas.** `KAM.Iso.Apost.correction` and `NewtonErrorAux.border_facts` are used only for their
  qualitative conjuncts, which take no constant hypotheses. Their old-constant bounds are discarded.
