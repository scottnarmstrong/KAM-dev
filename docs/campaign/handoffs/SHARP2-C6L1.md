## Approach

**1. The strip.**
- **Why a narrower strip.** `EntryHypA` asks for ℓ¹ norms on the route's whole strip `N.ρ`. The certifier encloses
  its objects in sup on `T_ρ`, and C5CORE's `Encloses.toL1` turns a sup enclosure on `T_ρ` into an ℓ¹ enclosure on
  `T_{ρ−δ₁}`. Only the remainder pays `cothPowUp n δ₁`; the polynomial part is exact.
- **What the route does.** It runs on `N.ρ = ρ' = L.W1.rho < ρ`, with `ρ∞` unchanged. Every sup hypothesis restricts
  to the smaller strip (`refHyp_of_le` is C6's `refHyp_of` for `N.ρ ≤ ρ`), and the conclusion lives on `T_{ρ∞}`.
  The torsion is taken on `T_{L.WT.rho}` with `ρ' − δ₀ ≤ WT.rho`.
- **The trade-off for a certificate.** `δ₁` costs about `4δ₁/(ρ − ρ∞)` relative in threshold, and multiplies the
  model remainders by `≈ (1/(πδ₁))ⁿ`.

**2. The ℓ¹ data from the models** (`RouteAL1Facts.lean`).
- `E`: `invarianceModel`, the landed invariance compose, one coordinate at a time. `invarianceModel_encloses` is the
  landed proof stated per coordinate.
- `L = DK`: `dkModels`.
- `N`: C4's `frameNModels` from the Gram witness.
- `T`: the torsion block of `torsionModels`.
- Each entry's bound is `l1Up W' C m`: the sup bound of the model with radius `C·rad` at the weight of `T_{ρ'}`.
- Holomorphy and periodicity of `E` and `T` come from the standing hypotheses at `sω*`, which `standingAt` builds
  from `BaseFacts` and the Diophantine premise.
- `ηᴺ = LᵀΩ₀E` and `ηᴸ = −NᵀΩ₀E` are bounded by Wiener products (B10a's `L1Mat.mulVec`): `a_N e ≥ |LᵀΩ₀|₁|E|₁`,
  `a_L e ≥ |NᵀΩ₀|₁|E|₁`. The frame option uses `|L|₁` and `|N|₁`.

**3. `Γ_∞`** (`RouteAL1Gamma.lean`).
- **Clock boxes.** For a clock box `[(c₀, r₀), (κ, 0)]` (C2's `clockOk`), `sω* = (sκ)(ω', 1)`.
- **The fibered test.** `fibCheck` is B10C's PlasmaLean checker with the interval of `ω'` as data. It is SHARP-L9's
  `smallDivisorSup_snoc_le`, one entry per fiber.
- **Scaling.** `smallDivisorSup_smul_le`: `Γ_∞(tω) ≤ Γ_∞(ω)/t`.
- **Scope.** Certified only for `n = 2`, `τ = 1`, as C2's sites are. Any other certificate fails `entryOk`, which
  is sound, only incomplete.

**4. The mirror** (`RouteAL1Q.lean`, `RouteAL1Run.lean`). B10C's `MirrorL1` was fixed-frequency at `τ = 1`. This one
is on C6's isoenergetic `StepQ`/`NumQ` at any `τ ∈ ℕ`:
- `e⁻¹` is replaced by `ei ≥ 0.3679`, checked in `okL1`;
- the constants without `e⁻¹` are exact casts; the others are monotone (`le_*`);
- soundness goes through downward closure and monotonicity of the errors and the cap sums.

**5. The branch, not the wrapper**, for C6's reason: `iso_g1_v2A_l1`'s displacement constants are maxima with v1's
`C₂♯`, which leaves no room on the ray near a refined threshold.

**6. R1.**
- **The strip.** R1's ℓ¹ strip is `ρ' = 1/70`, so its schedule `δ_s` is C6's and C6L8's and their bites apply
  unchanged. Its sup data are taken on `T_{1/70 + 1/10⁵}` (`r1ParamsL`). The R1 core does not read `params.rho`.
- **The witness.** Caps are the fixed point `cap = init + sum`. `X⋆ = 680000` and `ϑ = 1/32`; `ϑ` hardly matters.
- **What binds.** At `X⋆ = 700000` `J2` fails, as in C6L8; its value goes 0.366 → 1.053. The smallness binds at the
  witness.
- **The `Γ_∞` witnesses.** Computed by `scripts/apost_sharp/c6l1_gamma.py`, an exact mirror of `gamOk`:
  `Γ_∞(sω*, δ₀) ≤ 5.1732` (N = 377, `E₁ = 0.9852`) and `Γ_∞(sω*, ρ') ≤ 3.3839` (N = 43). The float values are
  5.163 and 3.377.

- **The R1 bites under the new cap.** The `l = 13` bite is above lemma.farm's 10 GB per-process limit, and the
  l ≤ 12 module was at 10.4 GB. So the R1 core cannot run unsharded there.
  - It needs one bite per process, plus a rounded `balBound` or a smaller cut-off for `δ₄`.
  - Prefix 4 drops the `l = 13` bite at a cost of 0.16 orders (−10.34).
- **Lunar-size ℓ¹ enclosures** (extrapolated, nothing measured on lunar data). `checkL1Claim` recomputes:
  - **`checkL1E`:** the invariance compose, the certifier's dominant cost, plus `2n` passes of `l1Up`. A pass costs
    the same as a `supBound`: negligible next to the compose.
  - **`checkL1T`:** `gramCheck` and `torsionModels`, the cost of `checkTorsion`, plus `n²` passes.
  - **`checkL1N`:** `gramCheck` and the `L·G⁻¹` model product, part of `checkFrameN`.
  - **`checkL1L`:** cheap.
- **Lunar estimate.** With C6's figures (one compose 0.8–2.2 CPU-h per torus at L ≈ 33, 0.3–0.9 at L ≈ 18), the
  ℓ¹ check adds about one compose plus one torsion pass: **≈ 1.6–4.4 CPU-h per torus at L ≈ 33, 0.6–1.8 at
  L ≈ 18**. Memory is that of the landed checks, since `l1Up` builds no new models. `routeAL1` also repeats C6's
  `checkHamClaim` and `checkFrameMajorants`. With a shared compose (C7's) the marginal cost of the ℓ¹ data is the
  `l1Up` passes only, seconds.
- **`Γ_∞` at lunar `δ₀`** is cheap with the fibered test: measured on R1, about 8 s and ≤ 4.1 GB. C5CORE's box
  test would need ≈ 2.8·10⁵ modes.
- **A lunar certificate also needs accurate models.** Its frame models (Gram witness radius, product truncation)
  must have remainders small against `|N|₁/(πδ₁)⁻ⁿ`, or a larger `δ₁`. Example: `δ₁ = 10⁻⁵` (the R1 core) has
  conversion factor ≈ 10⁹, so `N`'s remainder must be ≲ 10⁻¹¹ relative. With `δ₁ = 10⁻⁴`: 10⁷, at 0.012 orders.
