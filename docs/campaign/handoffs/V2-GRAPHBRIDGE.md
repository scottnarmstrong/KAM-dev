## Approach

**(a) The bridge.** `Confinement.GraphTorus V H E lo hi` asks for `k`, a continuous two-sided
inverse `Θ` of the angle map, the linear flow of `V`, the level and the action box.
`IsAnalyticAngleGraph` already carries a bijective angle map with analytic (hence continuous)
inverse, so `Θ = invFun (torusAngle k)` and the two inverse laws are `apply_invFun`,
`invFun_apply`; the flow, the level and the box are hypotheses (from `IsRealModelTorus` in the
`OfModel` forms: `flow`, `energy`; `V = X_H`, `ν = ω`). The near-identity adapter enters through
`IsAngleGraph.isAnalyticAngleGraph` (analyticity of `k` from `IsRealModelTorus.analytic` in the
model form), the clock adapter through the structure parent `IsClockAngleGraph.toIsAnalyticAngleGraph`.
The definitions are `def`s (`GraphTorus` carries data) in an `@[expose]` section, so the
confinement's `graph₁` computes through them (`toGraphTorus_graph₁ : graph₁ φ = torusGraph k φ 0`
by `rfl`).

**(b) Nits.**
* N1: §3.1 now says `SoGraph.graphAbs_sub_le` and `soGraphInv`/`lockInv` *correspond to*
  `abs_slice_le` and `invFun (torusAngle k)` (analogues on different carriers); the `rfl` claim
  for `soGraphMonoModel` stays.
* N2: the landed docstrings cannot be edited, so the `n ≥ 3` caveat is stated in the contract
  (§2.4 *Scope*, statement by statement, and in the module-map rows of `ClockGraphCert` and
  `ClockGraphCheck`) and in the module docstring of the new `GraphBridge.lean`.
  `CertifiedTorusAt.clock` (one clock) is fine for every `n`; `isClockAngleGraph`,
  `exists_clockGraph`, `exists_clockGraph_of_checks` (every `l ≠ i` a clock) have satisfiable
  hypotheses only for `n ≤ 2`.
* N3: the sheared torus. On the toy system of `Tests/Confinement` (`H = −a₂`, field
  `((0, −1), 0)`), the flat torus `a = (c, 0)` is parametrized by
  `shearK c θ = (θ + 3 sin(2πθ₀) e₁, (c, 0))`, angle map `A(θ) = (θ₀, θ₁ + 3 sin 2πθ₀)`: clock
  angle `0` (`A₀ = θ₀`), free angle `1` (`∂₁A₁ = 1`), off-diagonal `∂₀A₁ = 6π cos 2πθ₀`. The
  shear depends on `θ₀` only, so the lines `s ↦ shearK c (θ + s(0, −1))` are still the flow of
  the toy field. `shear_isClockAngleGraph : IsClockAngleGraph (shearK c) 1 0 1` (through
  `isClockAngleGraph_of_clock`), and `shear_not_derivNearId`: `DA(0) e₀ = e₀ + 6π e₁`, so
  `‖DA(0) − 1‖ ≥ 6π` and no `DerivNearId` with `L < 1` holds. A *certified* sheared torus is not
  available cheaply: for an integrable toy (T2) the exact torus at a Diophantine frequency has
  constant `U` (`DU ω = 0` with `ω` irrational), and a non-integrable certificate is a new
  certification run.
* N4: the core-namespace names `KAM.Certificate.CertifiedTorus.exists_clockGraph(_of_checks)`
  are left as they are (recorded).
