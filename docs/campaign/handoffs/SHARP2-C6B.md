## Approach

- **Mirror.** Only `sg` and `sQ` use a real exponent, so `gen_stepbq.py` copies the bodies of `ModConstantsB/Data.lean` textually over
  `ℚ` and writes one cast lemma each. The `if` branches go through `push_cast [apply_ite (Rat.cast : ℚ → ℝ)]`, since Mathlib has no
  `Rat.cast_ite`. `NumBQ` takes `τ ∈ ℕ` and any rational `a`.
- **List defaults.** `c`, `c'`, `cbar` are read with the tail constants as defaults, not `0`, because `NumB.Wf.sd` asks for positivity
  at every index.
- **`J1t`.** `okB` checks the tail condition at `X_kp`; `stepCondB_anti` gives all of `[0, X_kp]`.
- **`Θ`.** As in C6: `Θ̂ = σω · boxNormUp box`, the theorems are applied at `σh = Θ̂/‖ω*‖`, and `ray_lt_of_disp` recovers `sinf < σω`.
- **Certificates.** Each prefix stage has three sites (`δ_s`, the split bite, `min r_s 1`), each certified or classical. The tail is
  classical at `δ_kp` and `min r_kp 1`, and serves all smaller bites because the full bites decrease (`NumB.Wf.r_anti`).
- **Refined `RefHypB`.** `c_Th = 2 d_X`, and the new `norm_hamComm_sub_le_of` gives `c_DTh = 2 d_{2X}` from the claimed bound. The
  frozen `norm_hamComm_sub_le` only gives `2M`.
- **Reuse.** At `a = 2`, `δ_s` and `min r_s 1` are route A's bites and `δ_s/2 = δ_{s+1}`, so route B adds no new expensive bite to R1.
  C6's site theorems apply through rational equalities of the bites.

## Deviations

1. **`toyB`, not T2.** `NumB.Wf.ρ_le` requires `ρ ≤ 1` and T2 has `ρ = 9`. `toyB` is T2 on the strip `ρ = 1`, `ρ∞ = 1/100`, weight
   `540 ≥ e^{2π}`, with everything else unchanged. It passes the landed sub-checks, the Ham claim and the frame majorants. On a
   certificate with `ρ > 1`, `wfOk` is false, so route B fails closed.
2. **`c_Th = 2 d_X`, not a certified `c_Th`.** The certifier has no check of the commutator. On R1, 3.675 against the design's 1.911
   costs 0.15 orders.
3. **Prefix 3, not the design's 5,** and a classical split constant at `s = 2`. A certified one gains 0.02 and would need a new `l = 13`
   bite.
4. **The frozen variant uses the literal wrapper, the refined variant the branch.** The wrapper's `max {C₂♯, c2B}` is harmless at 10⁻¹⁹.
5. **`RefClaim.bT` is still checked,** by `checkFrameMajorants`, though route B derives its torsion cap. It costs compute only.
6. **`RunBWit.num` fixes `c_Th`, `c_DTh`** at twice `M₁`, `M₂`, though the theorems allow more. No test needs more.
7. **`NumB.cert_of_le` is an addition** to the audit's constructor; it is the form a checker consumes.
8. **`routeABV2` uses `routeARefined`,** not `routeAV2'` (L8′), which landed after my base. Adding it is one `orElse`.
