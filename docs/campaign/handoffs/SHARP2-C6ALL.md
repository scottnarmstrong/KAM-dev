## Approach

The chain reuses the inner engines of `routeAV2L1` and `routeABV2` unchanged, so every route's soundness is the existing
one through `EngineV2.orElse`, and no new mathematics enters. The toy file runs the whole certifier at `routeV2All`,
not at the single engines, so each check also exercises the witness nesting end to end.
