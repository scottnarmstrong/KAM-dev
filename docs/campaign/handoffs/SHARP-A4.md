## Approach (blueprint `bp:p2s:triangular`, `correction`, `newton-error`, `perturbation`, `step`)

- **`triangular_bounds`.**
  - The qualitative facts (holomorphy, periodicity, zero averages) come from `KAM.Apost.triangular`.
  - The two cohomological bounds come from `hC.sol`: on `T_{r−δ}` for `ξᴺ`, and on `T_{r−2δ}` for `ξᴸ`.
  - The rest uses the constant-free helpers `Triangular.norm_triXi0_le`, `norm_triXiN_le` and `norm_triGL_le`.
- **`correction_bounds`.**
  - `‖T‖ ≤ H_T` on `T_{r−δ}` comes from A3's `torsion_bound` together with `hTS`.
  - The data bounds come from `frame_basic`, `periodicity`, `exactness` and `frame_bounds`.
  - Everything is then written in `u = ε/(γδ^κ)` and `E = ε/(γ²δ^{2κ})`, and `absorb` moves `ε` and `u` into the
    larger scale (`γ ≤ γ̄`).
- **`newton_error_bounds`.**
  - The identity is put back together from `NewtonError.sumVec_invarianceError_newtonMap`, `alg_core`,
    `xi_equation`, `sMat_spec` and `newtonMap_mem`.
  - The quadratic term uses `M/2` through `NewtonErrorSharp.norm_quadRemainder_half` (`taylor_iteratedFDeriv` from
    `KAM.Strip.Taylor`).
  - `‖S⁻¹‖ ≤ 2` comes from `hsym`, and the total from `total_le`.
- **`perturbation_poly`.**
  - **(a)** The ball margin is `PerturbationAux.ball_subset_of_close`.
  - **(b), (c)** The frame on `T_{r−3δ}` is `frame_items_sharp`:
    - a Cauchy estimate for `K̃ − K`;
    - `G̃ − G = L̃ᵀ(L̃ − L) + (L̃ − L)ᵀL`;
    - Neumann with ratio `ϑ = 1/8`.
  - **(d)** `torsion_diff_real`:
    - Both torsions are written as torsion polynomials (A3's `torsion_identity`, using `fderiv_tangentMat_eq` at
      any `ω'`), and then split as `𝒯 = 𝒯_ab + 𝒯_F + 𝒯_c`.
    - `𝒯_ab` is handled by `prod5_sub_le` twice; the constants are checked in `arith_ab` (`= C_Δab D`, with
      `D = d/δ ≥ d`).
    - `𝒯_F` and `𝒯_c` are bounded by their sizes, with factor `1 + ϑ̂³`, and `C_Δc D + (1 + ϑ̂⁵) σ_B³ w_T Ω̄`.
      The Lagrangian defect `Ω̄` of `K` comes from `geometry_sep`.
  - **(e)** `norm_torsionAverage_sub_le_any` and `neumann_ratio` with `q = ϑ`.
  - **Audit-requested helpers.** The fixed audit asked for named helpers for the parts `perturbation_poly` omits.
    They exist:
    - `KAM.Sharp.norm_linMat_sub_le_M` (`‖Ã − A‖ ≤ M d`);
    - `KAM.Sharp.norm_transpose_tangentMat_le_vthh`;
    - the transpose of `L̃ − L` reuses `PerturbationAux.norm_transpose_tangentMat_sub_le` inside
      `frame_items_sharp`.
- **`step_spec`.** It follows `KAM.Apost.step`:
  - **`st-small`.** `step_small` turns `hsmall` into its seven consequences. `hβ` (`2d ≤ β`) and `β > 0` give
    `d < β`.
  - **`st-err`.**
    - `correction_bounds` gives `‖ΔK‖ ≤ d`, so `‖K̄ − K‖ ≤ d` via `NewtonError.newtonMap_of_mem`.
    - `newton_error_bounds` gives the new error.
    - The qualitative clause (a) uses the first conjunct of `KAM.Apost.correction_spec` (it has no constants) and
      the three helpers in `KAM/Apost/Step.lean`.
  - **`st-pert`.** `perturbation_poly` is applied with `K̃ = K̄`, `ω̃ = ω` and `ε̃ = C_E ε²/(γ⁴δ^{2a})`.
    - `n d/δ = rateD·U` and `C_B d/δ = rateB·U` hold with `U = ε/(γ²δ^{2κ+1})` (`div_delta_eq`, via
      `Real.rpow_add_one`).
    - `twistD ≤ C_DT·U` holds by `twistD_le_cDT` (`absorb`: `ε/δ ≤ γ̄²U` and `ε/(γδ^{κ+1}) ≤ γ̄U`). Together with
      `step_small`, this gives `σ_T·twistD ≤ ϑ`.
    - The inverse difference is then `≤ ϑ̂ σ_T² C_DT U = rateT·U`.
