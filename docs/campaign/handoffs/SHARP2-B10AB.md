## Approach

**B10a.**
- *Sup bounds.* An ℓ¹ map on `T_r` equals its Fourier series (`KAM.eq_fourierSum_of_summableWeighted`), and the series
  is bounded by its weighted norm (`KAM.norm_le_of_l1`). Matrices use row sums (`KAM.norm_le_iff_forall_rowSum`).
- *Wiener product.*
  - On the strip both factors are absolutely convergent Fourier series. Their product is the Cauchy product,
    reindexed by `cauchyEquiv : (k, l) ↦ (k + l, l)`.
  - A summable series has its coefficients as Fourier coefficients (`KAM.torusCoeff_fourierSum`). Hence the product
    coefficients are `Fourier.convolution`, and `Fourier.weightedNorm_convolution_le` gives `L1Mat.mulVec`.
  - This is the strip analogue of `KAM.Weighted.coeff_mul`.
- *Cauchy.*
  - Termwise differentiation of the Fourier series (`hasFDerivAt_tsum_of_isPreconnected`, with the bound of
    `Fourier.mul_exp_radius_sub_le`) gives `∂ⱼ Σ aₖχₖ = Σ aₖχₖ 2πi kⱼ`.
  - Then `Σⱼ 2π|kⱼ| e^{−2πδ|k|₁} = 2π|k|₁ e^{−2πδ|k|₁} ≤ 1/(eδ)`, which gives the row-sum factor `1/(eδ)` (sup norms:
    `n/δ`).
  - The transpose costs `p/(eδ)`. The antisymmetric part costs `n/(eδ)`, because its diagonal vanishes.
- *Design signal handled.* `hasSum_fderiv_fourierSum_single` timed out at default heartbeats. It was restructured into
  `hasFDerivAt_fourierSum` (which returns `HasFDerivAt ∧ Summable`) and `fderiv_term_apply_single`; no override.

**B10b.**
- *Solves.* `l1_cohR` packages SHARP-L9's vector clauses. `l1_cohR_real` uses the coefficient estimate at target
  radius `0` (`KAM.summableWeighted_solve_sup`, no `δ < r` needed) and `norm_fourierSum_real_le`.
- *The ray.* Each term of `Γ_∞` scales by `1/s`. If the terms are unbounded, both suprema are `0` (no Diophantine
  premise).
- *Defect identity.*
  - Both `Ω_K` and `−(Du − Duᵀ)` are holomorphic, periodic and of zero average (`KAM.Apost.exactness`).
  - Their ω-derivatives agree: B2's `fderiv_lagrangeMat_eq_etaN` (sign minus) for `Ω_K`, and symmetry of the second
    derivative (`fderiv_apply_comm_of_differentiableOn`) for `Du`.
  - The a-priori clause of `KAM.Apost.cohomological` with `H = 0` kills the difference. `KAM.periodic_bounded` gives
    the bounded data needed on a narrower strip.
  - The bound then follows from `l1_cohR` and `L1Vec.norm_jac_antisymm_le`.
