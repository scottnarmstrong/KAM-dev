## Approach
- `CohData`: one private lemma `CohData.of_bounds` turns the raw bounds `c/(γ δ^κ)·H` of
  `cohomological_russmann`/`_parseval`/`cohomological` (and their `_operator` versions) into the
  `c γ⁻¹ δ^{-κ} H` form (`rpow_neg`). `cR_pos` needs the case `τ = 0` (`0^0 = 1`) handled by `rpow_tau_pos`.
  `cohR ω g` is definitionally `cohSolutionPi ω g`. The `δ ≤ 1` hypothesis of the fields is unused by the instances
  (the underlying theorems hold for all `0 < δ < r`).
- `consts_pos`: a chain of `positivity` facts, one per constant. `consts_nonneg` (private) is the same chain with
  `0 ≤` and no `1 ≤ n`, used by `step_small` (whose signature has no `hn`).
- `absorb`: set `s = δ^κ ≤ δ` (`κ ≥ 1`, `δ ≤ 1`), `p = γ s ≤ γ̄ δ`; every exponent becomes a power of `s`, `δ`
  (`rpow_nat_mul'`, `rpow_add`), and each of the 14 inequalities is `E/D₁ ≤ c (E/D₂)` ⇐ `D₂ ≤ c D₁`
  (`div_le_mul_div`), with the 11 conditions proved by explicit `calc`s in `p, γ̄, δ`.
- `step_small`: with `X = ε/(γ⁴ δ^{2a})`, `𝔠_step X ≤ 1`; the `max` chain gives `b1..b6` (`le_max_*`), `absorb` gives
  `ε/δ ≤ γ̄⁴X` etc. **Design signal (heartbeats):** the first version (all constants unfolded in context, `nlinarith`)
  hit the 200000 limit. Fixed by `generalize`-ing the nine constants (`cStep`, `cT1`, …, `rateD`) to plain reals
  after extracting the facts, and `linarith only [...]` instead of `nlinarith`. No override used.
- `neumann_ratio`: ring-level private lemma `ring_neumann_ratio` (same proof as `KAM.neumann` with ratio `q`),
  specialised through `Matrix.nonsing_inv_eq_ringInverse`. `norm_mul_sub_mul_le`: `X'Y'-XY = X'(Y'-Y)+(X'-X)Y`,
  `Matrix.linfty_opNorm_mul`.
