## The theorem (target 1)

Stated for the normalized case `ω = Fin.snoc ω' 1`. `smallDivisorSum_smul` gives `S(cω, δ) = c⁻² S(ω, δ)` for the
reduction of a last component `c ≠ 0`. `snoc_one_two`/`snoc_one_three` turn `Fin.snoc ![a] 1` into `![a, 1]`, and
likewise in dimension 3.

```
smallDivisorSum_snoc_le_fiber
  (hω : IsDiophantine γ τ (Fin.snoc ω' 1)) (hγ : 0 < γ) (hτ : 0 ≤ τ) (hδ : 0 < δ)
  (hN : 2τ < 2πδ(N + 1)) (p : (Fin m → ℤ) → ℤ) (L L₀ : (Fin m → ℤ) → ℝ)
  (hp  : ∀ k ∈ modeBox m N, |k·ω' + p k| ≤ 1/2)
  (hL  : ∀ k ∈ modeBox m N, 0 < L k ∧ L k ≤ |k·ω' + p k|)
  (hL₀ : ∀ k ∈ modeBox m N, p k ≠ 0 → 0 < L₀ k ∧ L₀ k ≤ |k·ω'|)
  (E : ℕ → ℝ) (hE : ∀ j, e^{-2πδj} ≤ E j) :
  S(Fin.snoc ω' 1, δ) ≤ E 1 · π²/3
      + Σ_{k ∈ modeBox m N} (E(|k|₁ + |p k|) (L k)⁻² + E(|k|₁) ([p k ≠ 0] (L₀ k)⁻² + E 1 · C₀))
      + sdTail γ τ δ N
```

The proof, with `b = 2πδ`:
- **Fibering.** Every finite partial sum of `S` is split by the `k'`-coordinate (`Fin.init`):
  - `k·ω = k'·ω' + k_n` (`frequencyDot_snoc`);
  - `|k|₁ = |k'|₁ + |k_n|` (`modeNat_eq_init_add`);
  - a fiber is parametrized injectively by `k_n` (`eq_of_init_eq_of_last_eq`).
- **`k' = 0`.** All modes have `|k_n| ≥ 1`, so their sum is at most `e^{-b} ∑_{j≠0} j⁻² = e^{-b} π²/3`
  (`sum_inv_sq_int_le`, from Mathlib's `hasSum_zeta_two`).
- **`0 < |k'|₁ ≤ N`** (`sum_fiber_le`). The nearest term `j = p` keeps its full weight `e^{-b(|k'|₁+|p|)}` and is
  bounded by `L⁻²`. The term `j = 0`, when `p ≠ 0`, keeps its weight `e^{-b|k'|₁}` and is bounded by `L₀⁻²`. Every
  other term has `|j| ≥ 1`, so its weight is at most `e^{-b(|k'|₁+1)}`; the weights are then dropped, and these terms
  sum to at most `C₀ = 4 + π²/3` (`sum_inv_sq_ne_le`):
  - shift `y = x + p` with `|y| ≤ 1/2`;
  - on the far side of `−x`, `|y + i| ≥ |i|`, giving `ζ(2)`;
  - on the near side, `|y − 1| ≥ 1/2` gives 4, and `|y − i| ≥ i − 1` for `i ≥ 2` gives `ζ(2)`;
  - `y < 0` follows by reflection.
- **`|k'|₁ > N`.** These modes have `|k|₁ > N`, so the tail `sdTail γ τ δ N` of SHARP-L2
  (`sum_smallDivisorTerm_tail_le`, separation and shells in `ℤⁿ`) applies unchanged.
- **The Diophantine bound in a fiber** (`fiber_dist_ge`, as the brief asks). For `k' ≠ 0` and every integer `p` with
  `|k'·ω' + p| ≤ 1/2`: `|k'·ω' + p| ≥ γ ((3/2 + ‖ω'‖)|k'|₁)^{-τ}`. Take `k = (k', p)` and use
  `|p| ≤ |k'·ω'| + 1/2 ≤ ‖ω'‖|k'|₁ + 1/2`. It is not needed for the tail (the `ℤⁿ` tail is sharper), but it lets a
  checker sanity-check the data and gives the fibered analogue of the separation.
- **Data are O(1) per fiber.** `p` (the nearest integer), two lower bounds `L` and `L₀` (from rational enclosures of
  `ω'`; `L₀ = |p| − 1/2` is always valid), and `E_j` (e.g. `E₁^j` with `E₁ ≥ e^{-b}`). The box has
  `#{0 < |k'|₁ ≤ N}` entries in `ℤ^{n−1}`: `2N` for `n = 2`, `2N(N+1)` for `n = 3`.

## Numbers (target 2: `python3 -I scripts/apost_sharp/smalldiv_fiber.py`)

Conventions:
- **Exact `S`.** The enclosure of `smalldiv_sum.py` (SHARP-L2).
- **`B_inf`.** The fibered bound with a negligible tail.
- **`N (1 %)`.** The smallest `N` with `B(N) ≤ 1.01 S`. A `*` means 1 % of `S` is out of reach; then `N` is the
  smallest with `B(N) ≤ 1.01 B_inf`.
- **Data entries.** `#{0 < |k'|₁ ≤ N}`, compared with the SHARP-L2 box `#{0 < |k|₁ ≤ N}` at the same `N`.
- **`c_R(δ)`.** `(2π)⁻¹ 2^{n/2} √B γ δ^τ`, with `B = B(N)`.

**Lunar R1.** `ω = (ν − 1, 1)`, `γ = 1/42`, `τ = 1`, `δ₀ = 0.0023786`.

- **Dropped weights.** Their cost is 0.5 % at δ₀ and falls like δ. The dominant `k_n` are `≈ −k'(ν−1)`, so the
  weights kept on the nearest term are the important ones; the dropped weights only touch a background of size about

- **R1 threshold.** In the `thresholds.py` model with `c_R = 0.00583`:

- **Large δ.** For δ ≥ 1/8 the unweighted background `C₀` is 20–40 % of `S`: the weights `e^{-b(|j|−1)}` dropped on

  SHARP-L2 box is small anyway (62 terms at δ = 1).

  δ = 1. Keeping those two weights (the `E 1` and `L₀` terms) was needed.

## Notes for v2

- **Possible refinement.** A per-fiber window of exact terms `j ∈ {p − r, …, p + r}`, with a background constant
  depending on `r`, would bring T3 at large δ within 1 %. It is not needed for the lunar frequency, where the bound is
  within 0.5 % at δ₀ and better below.
- **Uniformity in δ.** `c_R(δ)` is flat for the lunar frequency (0.00581–0.00583 for δ ≤ δ₀). So a v2 interface can
  certify a few `δ_s = δ₀ 2^{−s}` with this theorem and use `cohConstR` (or the L2 general bound) beyond, as noted in
  SHARP-L2.

## Uncertainties

- **Double precision.** The numbers are double precision, and the exact `S` comes from SHARP-L2's enclosure. They are
