## Approach

- The transfer lemmas follow the blueprint's steps directly. Taylor is `KAM.taylor_iteratedFDeriv`, the Neumann step
  `KAM.Sharp.neumann_ratio`, the triple telescoping `KAM.Sharp2.RouteA.triple_sub_le`.
- **Average twist.** `neumann_step` uses `neumann_ratio` with `q = m t`, not `inv_le_of_ref`. So `t_A = 0` is not a
  separate case, as the design anticipated it might be: nothing needs `m < σ` strictly.
- **Integrability.** The averaged lemmas keep the signatures' integrability hypotheses. `Integrable.lean` discharges them
  from holomorphy alone (open domain, holomorphic Hamiltonian, holomorphic torus, invertible Gram matrix), with no
  `Standing` and at any frequency.
