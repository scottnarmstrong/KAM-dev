## The results

### Targets 1 and 2

- **The series.** `smallDivisorSum ω δ = ∑' k, smallDivisorTerm ω δ k`, where the term is
  `|k·ω|⁻² e^{-2πδ|k|₁}` for `k ≠ 0` and `0` at `k = 0`.
- **Summability.** For `(γ, τ)`-Diophantine `ω`, `γ > 0`, `τ ≥ 0` and `δ > 0`, the series is summable
  (`summable_smallDivisorTerm`). Every partial sum is bounded by Rüssmann's lattice bound `KAM.sum_inv_sq_exp_le`.
- **Comparison with Rüssmann.** `smallDivisorSum_le_russmann` gives `S ≤ 6(1 + 2^τ)γ⁻²(2τ/(eπδ))^{2τ}`, and
  `cohConstSum_le_russmann` gives `(2π)⁻¹ 2^{n/2} S^{1/2} ≤ cohConstR n τ / (γ δ^τ)`. So the new estimate is never
  worse than `cohomological_russmann`.
- **The estimate.** `cohomological_sum` and `cohomological_operator_sum` are `KAM.cohomological` and
  `KAM.cohomological_operator` with identical premises. Every bound clause (solution, vector, matrix, a-priori)
  reads `(2 * Real.pi)⁻¹ * Real.sqrt (2 ^ n) * Real.sqrt (smallDivisorSum ω δ) * A` (resp. `* H`).
  - **Normalization.** It is the brief's `(2π)⁻¹ 2^{n/2} √S · A`, with `2^{n/2}` written `√(2ⁿ)`.
  - **Weights.** With `ρ' = ρ − δ/2`, the corner Bessel factor carries `e^{4πρ'|k|}` and the small-divisor factor
    `e^{-πδ|k|}` per term. Squaring gives exactly `e^{-2πδ|k|₁}` in `S`.
- **Diophantine condition.** It is used only for summability (of `S` and of the solution's coefficients).

### Target 3: `smallDivisorSum_le_box_add_tail`

For `(γ, τ)`-Diophantine `ω`, `δ > 0` and a cut-off `N` with `2τ < 2πδ(N + 1)`:

```
S(ω, δ) ≤ ∑_{k ∈ modeBox n N} (L k ^ 2)⁻¹ * E (modeNat k) + sdTail γ τ δ N
```

- **Data for the finite part.** `modeBox n N` is `0 < |k|₁ ≤ N`. The checker supplies:
  - `L k`, with `0 < L k ≤ |k·ω|` on the box;
  - `E m`, with `e^{-2πδm} ≤ E m` for `1 ≤ m ≤ N`.
- **The tail.**
  `sdTail γ τ δ N = C(1 − e^{-b})(N+1)^{2τ}e^{-b(N+1)} / (1 − e^{-(b − 2τ/(N+1))})`, with `b = 2πδ` and
  `C = 2(1 + 2^τ)γ⁻²` (no dependence on `n`).
  - **Proof.** It reuses the separation/shell bound `S_m ≤ C m^{2τ}` (`sum_inv_sq_frequencyDot_le`) and the exact
    Abel summation `abel_finite` of the Rüssmann files.
    - The shells `m ≤ N` are empty.
    - For `m ≥ N + 1`, `m^{2τ}e^{-bm} ≤ (N+1)^{2τ}e^{-b(N+1)} r^{m−N−1}` with `r = e^{-(b − 2τ/(N+1))}`
      (`rpow_mul_exp_le_geom`, from `(1 + x)^{2τ} ≤ e^{2τx}`). This turns the sum into a geometric series.
    - The Abel boundary term tends to 0 as the cut-off grows (`ge_of_tendsto`).
  - **For a rational checker.** `sdTail_le` bounds `T_N` by `C b (N+1)^{2τ} E' (1 + c)/c`, for any
    `E' ≥ e^{-b(N+1)}` and `c = b − 2τ/(N+1)`.
- **Supplying `E` and `L`** (in the style of `KAM/Certificate/Arith`, read and not edited):
  - `E m` can be `E₁^m` with `E₁ ≥ e^{-b}`, where `E₁` is a rational upper bound obtained from
    `Transcendental.ratExpUp` and the `π` enclosure.
  - `L k` can be `|k·ω_c| − ∑|k_j| r_j` from a frequency box (`FreqEncloses`).

### Target 4: numbers (`python3 -I scripts/apost_sharp/smalldiv_sum.py`, double precision)

partial sum. The effective constant is `c_R(δ) = (2π)⁻¹ 2^{n/2} S^{1/2} γ δ^τ`, from the upper end. It is invariant

## Notes for the v2 designer

- **All δ at once.** A v2 instance needs a `c_R` valid for every loss the iteration uses (`δ_s = δ₀2^{−s}`, also
  `δ_s/2` in the torsion bound), at the moving frequency `s̄ω*`.
  - The ray is harmless: `c_R` scales as `1/s`.
  - The numbers show `c_R(δ)` flat for the lunar frequency. A Lean proof for all `δ ≤ δ₀` still needs either
    certificates at finitely many `δ_s` plus `cohConstR` beyond them, or a scaling lemma. The hybrid is natural:
    `ε_s` decays doubly exponentially, so later steps have large slack.
- **Certificate cost.** The full box at δ₀ is 3.7·10⁶ rational terms, too many for `decide +kernel`.
  - A resonant-only variant (not proved here) keeps, for each `k₁`, only the nearest `k₂`: 2,666 terms. It bounds
    every other term by `|k·ω| ≥ j − 1/2`, i.e. at most `π² e^{-b|k₁|}` per row.
  - For R1 at δ₀ it gives `104,392` against `F_N = 103,586` (0.8% loss).
  - It would be a small extra theorem in the same file.

## Uncertainties

- **Double-precision numbers.** The enclosures are rigorous up to floating point, and the `|k·ω|` values are far
  above the rounding error (`≥ 1/(42·2|k|) ≥ 10⁻⁶` vs `|k|·10⁻¹⁶`). They are not Lean-certified; the Lean route for
  a given `ω` is target 3.
- **The γ used.** It is the certified `γ = 1/42` (lunar) and `1/12` (T3), not the best constant of the frequency.
  `c_R(δ)` is linear in the `γ` used.
