# V2-PACKED-CONS-BRIDGE: the packed route's torus theorem about LG00's own constants

**The gap (AUDIT-V2-PACKED-PROD-1 C2, AUDIT-V2-PACKED-JETROUTE-1 C1).** kamx-pk-gen's packed route proves
`LG00P.<T>.pk_kamTorus` about kamx-packed's copies of LG00's constants: `LG00P.<T>.lg00Cert` (LG00's certificate
without advice and witnesses), `LG00P.lg00TorusWith`, `LG00P.lg00OmegaStar`, `LG00P.LGF`, … LG00's statements
(`lg00-app` `applications/lg00/LG00/<T>/Main.lean`: `lg00_kamTorus`, `lg00_kamTorusStatement : LG00TorusStatement
lg00Cert lg00OmegaStar lg00WindowTP`) are about LG00's own constants.

**The bridge.**
- **Same environment.** kamx-pk-gen's integration package `kam-lg00p` requires LG00's scratch package `kam-lg00`,
  so the copies and the originals live in one environment.
- **Equal by `rfl`.** Every field the statement reads (`params`, `H`, `D`, `K`, ω*, E*) is equal in the copy and the
  original by `rfl`; the copy differs only in its empty witnesses and advice.
- **So the proof is `LG00P.<T>.pk_kamTorus` itself.** LG00's statement, verbatim, follows from it by definitional
  unfolding. AUDIT-V2-PACKED-JETROUTE-1 §1 found this with a probe; the bridge is that theorem, under the names
  agreed with kamx-conf.

**Modules** (generated by `scripts/packed/cons_bridge_gen.py --tree=<T>` into `.lake/v2-packed-bridge/src`, outside
every package):

| module | contents |
|---|---|
| `LG00B.<T>.Main` | `LG00.<T>.lg00_kamTorus_packed`: the statement of LG00's `lg00_kamTorus`, verbatim (LG00's namespace and `open` line), `:= LG00P.<T>.pk_kamTorus`; `LG00.<T>.lg00_kamTorusStatement_packed : LG00TorusStatement lg00Cert ω* window`; `LG00.<T>.lg00_certifiedTorus_packed : CertifiedTorus lg00Cert ω*`. T″ also `LG00.TPP.lg00_hamBounds_packed : checkHamBounds lg00Cert = true` (kamx-conf's request) |
| `LG00B.TPP.Fields` | for `lg00_hamBounds_packed`: the copy's `params`, `ham`, `weightH`, `centre`, `radius`, `expOrder` are LG00's (`rfl`) |
| `LG00B.<T>.Check` (audit) | LG00's two statements, extracted verbatim from LG00's own `<T>/Main.lean` with its `open` line, elaborated against the bridge's theorems; `#print axioms` |

**The Hamiltonian bounds for T″** are by kamx-pk-gen's `checkHamBounds_congr`, not by unfolding. The probes (`TP/probes/`,
on T′'s ported `LG00P.TP.lg00_hamBounds`):
- **By unfolding** (`DefTP`): `checkHamBounds LG00.TP.lg00Cert = true := LG00P.TP.lg00_hamBounds` makes the kernel
  evaluate the check. It was killed at the 4 GB cap (OOM, 27 s).
- **By the congruence** (`DefB`): 1.95 CPU-s, 0.31 GB, standard axioms.
- **The torus theorem alone by unfolding** (`DefA`): 3.0 CPU-s, 1.14 GB, 28 ms of kernel time.

T″ needs the bounds because its packed route proves the ball bounds on the copy, so LG00's own T″ balls are never
built on this path.

**Build.**
- **No Lake** (`scripts/packed/cons_bridge_build.sh <T>`). Each module is compiled with `lean` in its own process,
  under 4 GB after the 20 GB anon wait.
- **Read only.** It reads kamx-pk-gen's built packages: the integration core `v2-packed-gen-int`, `kam-lg00p` and
  `kam-lg00`. Nothing is written into them.
- **Outputs:** the oleans go to `.lake/v2-packed-bridge/build/lib/lean` of this worktree. kamx-conf compiles its
  `LG00.Confinement.Theorem1` against that path; it imports `LG00B.TP.Main` and `LG00B.TPP.Main`.
- **A killed process counts as failed.** `/usr/bin/time` reports `exit 0` for a signal kill, so since the `DefTP`
  probe the runner rewrites the exit code when the cgroup records an OOM kill or the process ends by a signal. No
  earlier build in these studies was killed: their logs were checked.

- **The statements are LG00's.** `LG00B.TP.Check` elaborates `lg00-app`'s `lg00_kamTorus` and
  `lg00_kamTorusStatement` statements, extracted from its `TP/Main.lean`, against the theorems.
- **kamx-conf:** it recompiled against this `Main` (olean sha256 `949ad771550c328c…`). `lg00_kamTorusStatement_packed`
  plugs into its `lg00_confinement_of_tori`, with standard axioms.
- **Pins** (`TP/sha256.txt`): the generated sources, `Main`'s three olean files, the input oleans
  (`LG00P.TP.PK.MainJ`, LG00's `TP.Data.Cert`, `Model`, `Confinement.Main`, `Confinement.InitialPoint`) and
  `lg00-app`'s `TP/Main.lean`.
- **History.** The first T′ bridge (`989deddbc`) proved the same theorems through explicit field equalities and a
  congruence lemma: four modules of about 2 CPU-s each. After the audit, the orchestrator asked for the definitional
  form. The names, types and module path are unchanged.

**What this does and does not say.**
- The theorems are LG00's statements about LG00's constants (`LG00.TP.lg00Cert`, `LG00.lg00OmegaStar`,
  `LG00.Confinement.lg00WindowTP`), proved through the packed route.
- They do not use LG00's landed checker run: `LG00.TP.Main`, `lg00_check`. The packed proof's own premises (its
  chains, `LG00P.lg00Ham_jet` with the 6.07 GB solo exception, T′'s `Xfer` with LG00's Hamiltonian bounds) are
  kamx-pk-gen's and kamx-packed's, as audited there.
