# V2-PACKED-CONS-BRIDGE: the packed route's torus theorem about LG00's own certificate

**The gap (AUDIT-V2-PACKED-PROD-1 C2).** kamx-pk-gen's packed route proves `LG00P.<T>.pk_kamTorus` about
`LG00P.<T>.lg00Cert`. That is kamx-packed's copy of LG00's certificate, without advice and witnesses: a different
constant from LG00's `LG00.<T>.lg00Cert`, tied to it only by source text. LG00's statements (`lg00_kamTorus`,
`lg00_kamTorusStatement : LG00TorusStatement lg00Cert lg00OmegaStar lg00WindowTP`, `lg00-app`
`applications/lg00/LG00/<T>/Main.lean`) are about LG00's constants.

**The bridge (option (b)).**
- **Which fields matter.** `lg00TorusWith`, and with it `CertifiedTorus` and `LG00TorusStatement`, reads a
  certificate only through four things:
  - `params`;
  - `H = ham.eval`;
  - `D = certDomain weightH.rho centre radius`;
  - `K = emb.eval`.

  None of their types depends on the arithmetic type.
- **The copy's fields are LG00's.** The two certificates live in one environment: kamx-pk-gen's integration package
  `kam-lg00p` requires LG00's scratch package `kam-lg00`. So the bridge proves by `rfl` that the copy's `params`,
  `ham`, `weightH`, `centre`, `radius` and `emb` are LG00's (the kernel unfolds both constants down to the
  identical literals), and the same for `ω*`.
- **Congruence.** Equal `params`, `H`, `D` and `K` give equivalent statements, across the two arithmetic types
  (`CertFixed LG00P.lgP` and `CertFixed LG00.lgP`).
- **Result.** LG00's statements, about LG00's own constants, follow from the packed proof.
- **Not re-targeted (option (a)).** Re-targeting would rebuild the whole packed route on LG00's certificate. The
  bridge costs four modules of about 2 CPU-s each.

**Modules** (generated by `scripts/packed/cons_bridge_gen.py --tree=<T>`; outside every package, in
`.lake/v2-packed-bridge/src`):

| module | contents |
|---|---|
| `LG00B.Congr` (tree-free) | `lg00TorusWith_congr`, `certifiedTorus_congr` (two certificates, two arithmetic types, equal `params`/`H`/`D`/`K`); `lg00TorusWith_copy`: the copy's `LG00P.lg00TorusWith` is LG00's, by `Iff.rfl` |
| `LG00B.<T>.Fields` | `params_eq`, `ham_eq`, `weightH_eq`, `centre_eq`, `radius_eq`, `expOrder_eq`, `emb_eval_eq` (all `rfl`); `H_eq`, `D_eq`, `K_eq`; `omega_eq` |
| `LG00B.<T>.Main` | `LG00.<T>.lg00_kamTorus_packed` (the statement of LG00's `lg00_kamTorus`), `LG00.<T>.lg00_kamTorusStatement_packed : LG00TorusStatement lg00Cert ω* window`, `LG00.<T>.lg00_certifiedTorus_packed : CertifiedTorus lg00Cert ω*`; T″ also `LG00.TPP.lg00_hamBounds_packed : checkHamBounds lg00Cert = true`, from the copy's `LG00P.TPP.lg00_hamBounds` by kamx-pk-gen's `checkHamBounds_congr` (for kamx-conf: T″'s packed route proves the ball bounds on the copy, so LG00's own T″ balls are never built on this path) |
| `LG00B.<T>.Check` (audit) | LG00's two statements, extracted verbatim from LG00's own `<T>/Main.lean` with its `open` line, elaborated against the bridge's theorems; `#print axioms` |

`lg00_kamTorus_packed`'s clauses:
- clauses 1–2, the Hamiltonian identity and the energy level, come from LG00's own `lg00Ham_identity` and
  `decide +kernel`, as in LG00's `Main`;
- clause 3, the torus with its window, is `pk_kamTorus.2.2` carried over by the bridge.

**Build.**
- **No Lake** (`scripts/packed/cons_bridge_build.sh <T>`). Each module is compiled with `lean` in its own process,
  under 4 GB after the 20 GB anon wait. It reads kamx-pk-gen's built packages read only: the integration core
  `v2-packed-gen-int`, `kam-lg00p` and `kam-lg00`. Nothing is written into them.
- **Outputs:** the oleans go to `.lake/v2-packed-bridge/build/lib/lean` of this worktree. kamx-conf compiles its
  confinement module against that path.


- **The statements are LG00's.** `LG00B.TP.Check` elaborates LG00's `lg00_kamTorus` and `lg00_kamTorusStatement`
  statements, extracted from `lg00-app`'s `TP/Main.lean`, against the bridge's theorems.
- **Pins** (`TP/sha256.txt`): the generated sources, the input oleans (`LG00P.TP.PK.MainJ`, both certificates,
  both `Model`s, LG00's `Confinement.Main` and `Physical.HamIdentity`) and `lg00-app`'s `TP/Main.lean`.

**What this does and does not say.**
- The theorems are LG00's statements about LG00's constants (`LG00.TP.lg00Cert`, `LG00.lg00OmegaStar`,
  `LG00.Confinement.lg00WindowTP`), proved through the packed route.
- They do not use LG00's landed checker run: `LG00.TP.Main`, `lg00_check`. The packed proof's own premises (its
  chains, `LG00P.lg00Ham_jet` with the 6.07 GB solo exception, `Xfer`'s import of LG00's Hamiltonian bounds) are
  kamx-pk-gen's and kamx-packed's, as audited there.
