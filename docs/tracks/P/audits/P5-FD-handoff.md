## Approach (nodes `bpP:p5:freq-data`, `stepP:p5:fd-bd/-lip/-beta`)
`freq_data` takes `rho = min rhob (betaRadius d m)` (`rhob, c0, M0` from `bilip`; `m` from the compactness
lemma; `r1, CQ` from `uniform_remainder`; `Cdio = dioConstant d (↑(a+b)+1)` from `dio_one`),
`LB = max 1 (2/m)`, `AN = LN = lipConst d` (one data constant serves both, since `lipConst ≥ jpBound`),
`LP = max 1 (MP * coth(δ/4)^a)` and `muStar = min (min 1 mu0) (min (c0/(2Λ)) (c0/(4Λ²LB)))`, giving
`muStar ≤ 1`, `muStar ≤ mu0` (F4) and the three smallness inequalities (the third strictly). Radius-2 bounds
(F3) are by Cauchy estimates on polydiscs of radius `δ/8` for `jetPolynomial` as a function of `J`
(fast radius `δ/16`, slow radius `< 2`, balls have slow radius `< 3`).

**Deviation from the source proof (not from any signature):** the source obtains `L_β` by an adjugate/Cramer
bound with `det ≥ B/2` after shrinking `ρ_c`. I use compactness of `[0,mu0]×closure V×S¹` for the lower
bound at real points and then perturb by the `I`-Lipschitz bound of `jetBeta`; the conclusion (`‖v‖ ≤ LB‖β v‖`
with a data-only `LB ≥ 1`) is the one in `PGAnalyticSpec`. Cramer is not used (so no Cramer lemma is exported).
