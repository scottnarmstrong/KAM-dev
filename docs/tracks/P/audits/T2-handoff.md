## Approach
- orbit_analytic: composition of `analyticOnNhd_keplerLong`, cos/sin, sqrt (positive radicand), division by 1+k.
- orbit_poincare_bridge: `poincare_facts` gives K>0 and planeSq v = 1-K^2, so sqrt(1-planeSq v)=K; prefactor L^2/(1^2 L^2)=1; poincareF unfolds definitionally.
- orbit (jet): along each line v=t w, with y = i(F-λ) the regular Kepler equation becomes y = t(A e^y - B e^{-y})/2, A=ζ̄X, B=ζX⁻¹. The degree-4 solution `aJet` (found by CAS, but verified in Lean as a fixed point of the jet map) is shown to approximate y to O(t^5) by a contraction argument; sqrt(1-t²c) and 1/(1+sqrt) get explicit O(t^5) bounds; the orbit jet is assembled in the `Jet5` algebra and `oJet = refJet` is closed by `linear_combination` with X·X⁻¹=1. Then O(t^5) along all lines + analyticity of both sides gives equality of iterated derivatives (all-direction, via power-series diagonal and polarization). No numerical/CAS output is used as a fact; CAS was only used to find the polynomial coefficients and the `linear_combination` multipliers that Lean then checks.
- Dependency note: `OrbitJet/Polar.lean` imports `KAM.Planetary.Nondegeneracy.DiagonalTensor` (only for `f_pol`, a non-provider implementation module).
