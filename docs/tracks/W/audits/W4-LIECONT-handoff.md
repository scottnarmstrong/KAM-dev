## AUDIT FOCUS

1. **Faithfulness of "continuous polynomial of degree `≤ 2`".** `IsHomContQuadPoly f` means
   `f v = c + L v + B v v` with `L` continuous linear and `B` continuous bilinear on the Banach space
   `ℂ² × ℓ^N_{a,p} × ℓ^N_{a,p}`. This is the standard notion. It implies the blueprint's
   "polynomial of degree `≤ 2` in `(y, z, z̄)`", and it also gives joint continuity in
   `(y, z, z̄)`, which is stronger than the blueprint's "continuous in `(z, z̄)`". The separate
   per-`y` continuity in `(z, z̄)` is the third conjunct. Please check that the definition is not
   vacuous: the witnesses `c, L, B` must be built from the components, and they are (see 3).
2. **Premises.** The premises are `HomFreqHyp` at `ξ ∈ Π`, `a, p ≥ 0`, and `F ∈ 𝒯_{s'}`. They are
   the same as in the landed `homLemma1_equation_all_gen`: `HomFreqHyp` is the weaker `j^{1/2}`-tail
   form, and `HomFreqHyp1.toHyp` converts. `ω = ω ξ`, and the carrier
   `homOmegaCarrier_of_bound … (homOmega_le h hξ)` is the composed carrier of that export. The
   `_gen` form also takes `1 ≤ p + p_+`, as `homLemma1_equation_all_gen` and
   `homLemma1Complete_*_gen` do. It is automatic at `p_+ = p+1`, so the W4 form does not have it.
   The blueprint's `θ ∈ T_{s'}` was dropped: the result holds for every `θ`, which is stronger.
   Since `HomOmegaCarrier` is a `Prop`, `homLieN_gen` and `homWeakBracketN_gen` do not depend on
   the carrier proof, so the statements cover any carrier evidence.
3. **Proof.** `L_N F = (2π)⁻¹ Σ_l ω_l ∂_{θ_l}F − i⟨F_z, Ωz⟩₀ + i⟨F_{z̄}, Ωz̄⟩₀`
   (`homLieN_gen_eq`). `∂_{θ_l}F` is: a constant, plus `Σ_k (∂b)_k y_k` (linear in `y`), plus two
   pairings `homPairL2 (D c)` (continuous linear), plus three `homPairL2 (D (M ·)) ·` terms
   (continuous bilinear via `ContinuousLinearMap.bilinearComp`). Under `|Ω_j| ≤ (1+M₀)(j+2)` the
   Ω-pairing is `homPairL2 (homDiagW_gen … x) u` (landed `homPairOmega_eq_clm_gen`, step
   `stepW:w4:liebd`). `F_z = c⁺ + 2Pz + Qᵀz̄` and `F_{z̄} = c⁻ + Qz + 2P'z̄` are affine continuous,
   so each Ω-pairing is linear plus bilinear. `⟨⟨N,F⟩⟩ = −L_N F` holds pointwise
   (`homWeakBracketN_eq_neg_lieN_gen`), so `⟨⟨N,F⟩⟩` is in the class too.
4. **The model.** `z, z̄` are stored representatives `ẑ ∈ ℓ²` (`ẑ_j = ω_{a,p}(j+2) z_j`), the
   landed W4 convention. The map `ℓ^N_{a,p} → ℓ²` is a linear isometry, so continuity and the
   polynomial structure transfer to `ℓ^N_{a,p}`.
