## Approach (item 5, a direct derivative computation)
Notation: `Φ = (embedded Q, embedded P)`. Along `τ ↦ w + τ h` the rotation is `rot3 a b c` with the explicit
`3×3` matrix of the tilt `t = (a, b, 0)`, `a = u/(2√G)`, `b = -v/(2√G)`, `c = √(1-a²-b²)`; its entry derivatives
are `rot3Deriv` (`rot3_hasDerivAt`, `spatialRot_line_hasDerivAt`). `rot3_deriv_eq` proves `dR = R [ω]ₓ` with
`ω = 2(c da - a dc, c db - b dc, b da - a db)` (certificates by hand/sympy, `linear_combination`). Then
`DΨ h = (R (Dq_h + ω_h × Φ_q), R (Dp_h + ω_h × Φ_p))` and `symplectic_form_alg` (pure algebra, `ring`) gives
`Ω_planar + ω_k·d(Φ_q×Φ_p)_h − ω_h·d(Φ_q×Φ_p)_k − (ω_h×ω_k)·(Φ_q×Φ_p)`. `Φ_q×Φ_p = G e₃` and its derivative is
`dG e₃` (`planarDeriv_cross`), `Ω_planar = Ω(spatialPlanar h, spatialPlanar k)` by `poincare_symplectic`, and
`rot3_form_identity` (one `linear_combination`) yields `ω_k3 dG_h − ω_h3 dG_k − G(ω_h×ω_k)_3 = du_h dv_k − dv_h du_k`.
Numerically checked beforehand (finite differences, pullback matrix `dI∧dλ + dη∧dξ + du∧dv`).
