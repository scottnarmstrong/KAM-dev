## AUDIT FOCUS
- `StrongTubeData`: that its fields say what MAIN L8 line 253 says. In particular
  (a) sup bounds are on the half tube `T (R/2)`, derivative bounds on `T (R/4)`, denominators on `T ρ` with
  `ρ ≤ R/4`, so every statement holds on the domain (22) (all inside `T ρ`); this is stronger than "a slightly
  smaller domain" and costs nothing. (b) `M` bounds `fc`, `M'` bounds `Ψc` separately (MAIN's single `M` is
  the max). (c) "single holomorphic branch" is the principal branch of `(w·w)^(1/2)` on `Re (w·w) > 0`,
  checked on real points to be the Euclidean length (`star_real`, `pair_real`).
- `cDot` is bilinear (`∑ v_k^2`), and `cBody` extracts block `i` of a complex coordinate vector exactly as
  `bodyVector` does (`finProdFinEquiv (i, k)`); `re_cDot_ge` is stated coordinatewise in the sup norm
  (MAIN uses the Euclidean `η_q`; sup ≤ Euclidean, so this is the same estimate with `Q` a coordinate bound).
- `exists_tube_re_cDot_ge`: the constant bookkeeping `ε = min 1 (d²/(4+8Q))`, `Q = 2 max R 0`,
  `ρ = min (R'/4) (ε/2/(C'+1))`, with `C' = max C 0`. The pair vectors use `|u_k| ≤ 2R`, coordinate error `2·(ε/2)`.
- `exists_strongChart`: the radius is the `exists_pos_le_half` radius for the `4n` coordinate tubes (a factor 2
  smaller than the minimum, harmless), and the real-trace clause also delivers `w ∈ chartSafe ν`.
- `lemma8_complete`: `rStar ≤ 4*r` (N3) and the use of D5 with `R := 2ρ`; hypotheses (22) are copied from D5.
- Helper theorems with property hypotheses (`exists_tube_re_cDot_ge` takes `IsChartExtension`, a derivative bound
  and real barrier bounds as typing data): they are used only inside the proof of
  `exists_strongDenominatorTube`/`exists_strongTubeData`, where all of them are proved before use; the public
  summary theorems take only source premises (`hν hn hL hLch hg hδ`).
